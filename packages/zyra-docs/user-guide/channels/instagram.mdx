title: Instagram channel
description: Connect an Instagram account to Zyra and run comment-to-DM campaigns with follower reporting.

Zyra can connect to an Instagram account so you can reply automatically to comments with a direct message (DM) and track how your follower count evolves.

## Before you start

- You need access to your workspace **Settings**.
- You need the Instagram account you want to connect and permission to authorize it when Instagram asks.
- The Instagram connection must be configured on your server. If you see "Instagram connection is not configured on this server", ask your administrator.

## Connect your account

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Go to **Accounts** and choose **Instagram**.
3. If no account is connected yet, use the option to add a new account, then click **Connect with Instagram**.
4. Sign in and authorize on Instagram. You are sent back to Zyra when it finishes.

If the connection fails, Zyra shows "Failed to connect the Instagram account." Try again, or ask your administrator.

## How it works

Once connected, the Instagram page has three tabs: **Campaigns**, **Reports** and **Diagnostics**.

### Campaigns (comment to DM)

A campaign sends a direct message when someone comments one of your keywords.

1. In **Campaigns**, click **New campaign**, or start from a ready-made template (or **Start from scratch**).
2. Fill in the **Campaign name**, the **Keywords**, and the **Direct message reply**. You can also add **Public reply variations**.
3. Choose a **Post**, or turn on **Attach automatically to the next Reel**.
4. Optionally require the person to follow the account before receiving the DM, and add a **Follow-up message** with a **Follow-up delay (minutes)**.
5. Click **Create campaign**.

You can enable or disable, edit, duplicate or delete each campaign. **Import CSV** creates several campaigns at once, using the columns `keywords` and `replyMessage` (separate keywords with `;` or `|`).

### Reports

**Reports** shows how your follower count changes over time. You can turn on **Share report publicly** and use **Copy public report link** to share it.

### Diagnostics

**Diagnostics** shows the health of the connection: access token status (Valid, Expiring soon, Expired or Unknown), last webhook received, last reconciliation, and pending and failed jobs.

## Good to know

- Follower data appears only after it has been captured. Until then you will see "No follower data captured yet".
- If the token is expiring or expired, reconnect the account from the same page.
- Anyone with the public report link can view the report, so share it carefully.
- This page does not say how received messages are stored in the CRM. Ask your administrator if you need that.
