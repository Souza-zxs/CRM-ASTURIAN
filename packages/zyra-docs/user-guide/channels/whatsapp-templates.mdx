---
title: WhatsApp message templates
description: Create WhatsApp templates, submit them to Meta for approval and send them from a workflow.
---

Templates are pre-written messages that Meta must approve. They are required to message contacts outside the 24-hour window.

## Before you start

- A connected WhatsApp number (see [WhatsApp channel and AI agent](/user-guide/channels/whatsapp)).
- The WhatsApp features enabled on your instance. If the card is missing, ask your administrator.

## Create and submit a template

1. Open **Settings > User > Accounts** and choose **WhatsApp Templates**.
2. Click to create a template (**New WhatsApp template**).
3. Fill in the form:
   - **WhatsApp number**
   - **Name**: lowercase letters, digits and underscores only. This is Meta's internal identifier.
   - **Category**: Marketing, Utility or Authentication.
   - **Language**, for example `pt_BR`.
   - **Header** and **Footer** (both optional).
   - **Body**: use `{{1}}`, `{{2}}` for variables, for example: `Hi {{1}}, your session opens in {{2}} minutes.`
   - **Buttons** (optional): use **Add button** and set a label, plus a URL or phone number where applicable.
4. Click **Create template**. It is saved as a draft.
5. In the template row, click **Submit for approval**. The template becomes Pending.
6. Later, click **Refresh status** to fetch Meta's decision.

## Use it in a workflow

In a workflow, add the action that sends a WhatsApp template and set:

- the template to send;
- **to**: free text or a variable such as `{{trigger.phones.primaryPhoneNumber}}`;
- **body parameters**: values for `{{1}}`, `{{2}}`... in order.

## How it works

Submitting sends the template to Meta through your connected number. Refreshing reads the current status from Meta and stores any rejection reason.

## Good to know

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Created, not yet submitted. Editable. |
| Pending | Submitted, waiting for Meta. Not editable. |
| Approved | Ready to send. |
| Rejected | Refused by Meta. Editable and can be resubmitted. |

- Only Draft and Rejected templates can be edited or submitted. Editing a rejected one returns it to Draft.
- A workflow step fails if the template is not Approved.
- Deleting a template is permanent.
